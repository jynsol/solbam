# Festive art and effects v2

The original `trees/large_green.png` remains the centerpiece. Its silhouette, snow and pixel texture are unchanged. Runtime lighting, burgundy ribbon swags, bows and small hanging stars decorate that existing tree. A separately generated detailed tree concept was rejected and is not loaded or committed.

`elf-christmas-v2.png` replaces the tree monster with a Christmas house elf boss. Built-in ImageGen used the approved Santa as the direct style reference. Final prompt direction: one front-facing house elf with exactly the Santa's coarse low-resolution grid, thick stepped brown outlines, short chibi proportions, plain vertical dark eyes without highlights, small block cheeks, no mouth, simple cream fringe, small pointed ears, bent green cap with red cuff and gold square bell, green tunic with cream collar, red belt, short stockings and brown shoes. Flat limited palette, no texture, gradients, detailed hair, wand or background. Export: 72×84 transparent PNG, rendered at 24×28 logical units, nearest-neighbor scaled, hard alpha and 48 colors.

Forest restoration adds lights, connected overhead garlands, baubles, ribbons and hanging stars. Ground gift boxes are removed. Restoration wisps carry light upward from the player to tree branches. A few deterministic golden motes animate around restored trees.

Combat uses tapered gold/ivory/cranberry ribbons, mint trailing arcs, star fragments, snow crystals and expanding star-ring impacts. Finisher flash/shake is reduced so effects remain visible. Fragment colors match cookie/snow/elf artwork. The three monsters, player, Sol and Bambi still comprise the opening/ending/invitation cast.

Validation: JavaScript syntax; native Canvas opening/ending, all encounters and hit flashes, party reset/bounds, portraits/card, four restoration levels, skill effects and upward restoration particles. Existing player animation assertions pass. No physical-device/browser interaction test was performed.
